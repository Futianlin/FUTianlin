I am **Tianlin Fu**, a PhD researcher in the [Robotics and Mechatronics (RAM) group](https://www.ram.eemcs.utwente.nl/) at the **University of Twente**, Faculty of Electrical Engineering, Mathematics and Computer Science (EEMCS). Within RAM, I am a member of the [Robotic Vision and Intelligence (RVI) group](https://www.rvirobotics.com/), led by [Dr.ir. Kenan Niu](https://research.utwente.nl/en/persons/kenan-niu/).

My PhD research is supervised by Dr.ir. Kenan Niu and [Prof.dr.ir. Stefano Stramigioli](https://www.utwente.nl/en/research/our-scientists/stramigioli/).

My research interests lie at the intersection of **human–robot interaction, human-centered sensing, and intelligent robotics**. I am particularly interested in how robots can better understand human intentions and needs, collaborate with people, and adapt their behavior in different interaction contexts.

My broader interests include physiological sensing and multimodal interaction, particularly EEG and surface electromyography (sEMG), as well as computer vision, robot learning, and multi-agent systems.

---

## Research Interests

- **Human–Robot Interaction:** Human–robot collaboration, shared autonomy, and human-centered interaction.
- **Human-Centered Sensing and Interfaces:** Brain–computer interfaces (BCIs), EEG, sEMG, and multimodal physiological sensing.
- **Robot Intelligence and Learning:** Computer vision, representation learning, and reinforcement learning.
- **Multi-agent Systems:** Intelligent agents, coordination, and collaborative robotic systems.
